**Observation window:** 17 November 21:00 to 18 November 21:00, Asia/Riyadh. Sources are the running Jetson containers' retained status logs and, where marked, a separate live subscription to Rules. All counts and timings below were measured on the edge. The production row builder in this period used the 32-slot input queues.

## Queue sizing

At four cameras × 12 FPS, each of the event and alert streams receives about 48 **batches**/s. A frame with 500 accepted events still consumes one event-batch slot. With an initially empty queue and a completely stopped worker, 32 slots buy about **0.67 s**; 512 buy **10.67 s** and 1,024 buy **21.33 s** on slot count alone. The deployed RAM queue also expires inputs after **5 s** and has a **4 MiB per-input byte limit**. Increasing slots alone cannot deliver 10–21 s of retention. A downstream delay matters here only once it prevents the worker from draining its input.

The proposed 100 MiB SQLite inbox is shared across input streams and has no five-second expiry. Its hold time depends on free bytes divided by net incoming payload bytes/s. These deployed measurements establish neither a requirement for 512 or 1,024 slots nor sustained Jetson capacity at 400–500 **events per frame**. For 500 events on every frame of one 12 FPS camera, the input alone is 6,000 events/s before alerts.

**Coverage:** Camera and buffer samples span the 24 hours to within approximately five seconds of the boundaries. Shorter retained periods are stated for inference, Rules and row builder. Maximum spacing between retained samples was 6.12 s or less; no decrease was observed in the primary frame/batch/row counters used for rates. This report describes the specified deployment and traffic.
